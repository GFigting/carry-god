# /cg-optimize

## Purpose

Improve a measurable performance, cost, or workflow-efficiency metric.

## Route

1. Name the metric, baseline, target, and measurement method.
2. Locate the dominant bottleneck using current evidence.
3. Compare candidate approaches and their trade-offs.
4. Apply one bounded optimization at a time.
5. Measure before and after under comparable conditions.
6. Keep the change only when the target improves without violating constraints.

## Preferred Capabilities

- `gstack/benchmark`
- `ce-optimize`
- `gstack/plan-eng-review`
- `skills/improve-codebase-architecture`

## Output

Baseline, optimization hypothesis, implementation, before/after measurements, and residual trade-offs. Without a measurable target, route to `/cg-refactor` or `/cg-work` instead.
